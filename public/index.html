Key Advantages

Why SmartTrip?

Built as an intelligent decision-support system, not a simple CRUD database

Personalized Planning

Customizes visits based on specific weights for History, Food, Photography, Nature, Adventure, and Culture.

Budget Optimization

Automated breakdown of transportation, stays, dining, and activities with smart cost-saving suggestions if overbudget.

Smart Recommendations

Scores destinations out of 10.0 using interest alignment, popularity index, budget compatibility, and opening hours.

Route Optimization

Greedy Nearest Neighbor heuristic calculates Haversine distances to order stops sequentially and minimize travel time.

Group Consensus Engine

Allows multiple travelers in a party to vote on interests, aggregating weights to ensure everyone's favorite activities are included.

Rainy Day Backup Plan

One-click alternative itinerary generator that dynamically switches outdoor walks to museums, indoor galleries, and cafes.
